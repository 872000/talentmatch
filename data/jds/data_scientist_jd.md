# Data Scientist — InsightLoop

**Location:** Toronto, ON (hybrid) · **Type:** Full-time

## About the role
Join our data science team to build machine learning models that drive product
decisions. You'll run A/B testing programmes and communicate with data
visualization.

## Requirements
- 3+ years with Python, Pandas, NumPy, scikit-learn.
- Deep learning exposure (PyTorch or TensorFlow).
- Strong statistics background; A/B testing in production.
- Data visualization with Tableau or Power BI.
- SQL fluency; experience with Spark or Airflow a plus.
- Git-based workflow; Agile team experience.

## Nice to have
- ETL pipelines, dbt, AWS.
